Logos Storage Roadmap

Roadmap Overview

The Logos Storage team is working towards these features, required for mainnet:

  1. Logos Core Integration
  2. Scalable, Simple Filesharing
  3. Privacy-Preserving Filesharing

In addition, the team is also working on the following post-mainnet features:

  1. Research on Incentivized, Anonymous Persistence

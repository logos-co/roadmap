Key Highlights

Messaging

Logos Delivery v0.39.1 released and taken up by the delivery module; offline backfill is now one topic-aware engine covering both live-delivery outages and restarts (#4468 … #4474).

AnonComms

Logos Core

One signing interface — a shared logos.signer/1 interface and a signer manager; the keystore, Keycard and Keycard Shell are now signers behind it (signer-interface #1, signer-manager #1).

Storage

Blockchain

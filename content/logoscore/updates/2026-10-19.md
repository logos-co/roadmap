# Logoscore Weekly Update — 2026-10-19

## Highlights

- **Sign with hardware and air-gapped wallets in Basecamp.** Keycard, on a card reader or as a Keycard Shell over USB, and QR wallets such as Keystone, Passport, Jade and SeedSigner now sign Basecamp transactions. An account held by more than one of them shows up once, and you choose which one signs. (merged, not yet in the app catalog) [logos-qr-signer-module#1](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-qr-signer-module/pull/1), [logos-keycard-ui#3](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-keycard-ui/pull/3), [logos-signer-manager-module#11](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-signer-manager-module/pull/11)
- **The Bitcoin wallet can sync from any Bitcoin Core node.** (merged, not yet in the app catalog) [logos-bitcoin-wallet-core-module#1](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-bitcoin-wallet-core-module/pull/1), [logos-bitcoin-wallet-ui#1](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-bitcoin-wallet-ui/pull/1)

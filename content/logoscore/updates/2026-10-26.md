# Logoscore Weekly Update — 2026-10-26

## Highlights

- **Add Ethereum testnets to your wallet from Basecamp.** The Ethereum RPC app now lets you show mainnets, testnets or both, and switch each chain on or off. You can add Sepolia to the wallet without leaving the app. (released in the app catalog as `eth_rpc_ui` 0.1.1) [logos-eth-rpc-ui#8](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-eth-rpc-ui/pull/8), [logos-modules-release#122](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-modules-release/pull/122)

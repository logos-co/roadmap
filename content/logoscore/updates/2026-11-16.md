## This week, technically

| Area | What changed | Status | PRs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sending payments with your own node | With your own node chosen, each wallet now asks where payments go, per network: through public servers over Tor, or through your node. The default follows the node. If the node already sends over Tor (Bitcoin's private broadcast, Monero's `txProxy`), payments go through it. Otherwise they go through public servers over Tor. In Zcash the choice now also covers your own server at an address, and "In Logos" is the default. A Bitcoin send through your node shows as seen only once it is in the node's mempool. Before, a send that was still in private broadcast was shown as seen | merged; not in the catalog | [logos-bitcoin-node-module#9](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-bitcoin-node-module/pull/9), [logos-bitcoin-wallet-core-module#36](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-bitcoin-wallet-core-module/pull/36), [logos-bitcoin-wallet-backend#14](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-bitcoin-wallet-backend/pull/14), [logos-bitcoin-wallet-ui#31](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-bitcoin-wallet-ui/pull/31), [logos-monero-node-module#13](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-monero-node-module/pull/13), [logos-monero-wallet-core-module#19](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-monero-wallet-core-module/pull/19), [logos-monero-wallet-backend#13](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-monero-wallet-backend/pull/13), [logos-monero-wallet-ui#19](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-monero-wallet-ui/pull/19), [logos-zcash-wallet-ui#23](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-zcash-wallet-ui/pull/23) |
| Per-network Tor, follow-ups | The Zcash wallet gets the per-network "Use Tor for", the network picker in the header and the Node popup that Bitcoin and Monero got last week. Monero stagenet and testnet now catch up from a second operator, so one operator no longer sees both the direct catch-up and the Tor traffic. A Monero network now uses Tor only when it chose it or a wallet asked on it, which fixes a 15 s stall when switching networks. The Monero header says "catching up directly" during a catch-up, and the Zcash copy now says what the core checks and which server takes over | merged; not in the catalog | [logos-zcash-wallet-ui#21](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-zcash-wallet-ui/pull/21), [#22](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-zcash-wallet-ui/pull/22), [logos-monero-node-module#12](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-monero-node-module/pull/12), [#14](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-monero-node-module/pull/14), [logos-monero-wallet-ui#18](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-monero-wallet-ui/pull/18), [logos-monero-wallet-core-module#18](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-monero-wallet-core-module/pull/18), [logos-monero-wallet-cli#9](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-monero-wallet-cli/pull/9) |

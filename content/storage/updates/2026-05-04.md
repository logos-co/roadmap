## Week 18 2026 (Mon Apr 27 - Fri May 1, 2026)

### Highlights

* We've completed the LIONESS specification for Mix payload encryption: https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/329. This should provide the strong guarantees for payload integrity that we are looking for.
* Our PR which adds the dial-back address to AutoNAT v2 has been merged in libp2p: [libp2p/nim-libp2p#2354](https://github.com/vacp2p/nim-libp2p/pull/2354). You can now get the observed address as part of the callback, and use it to e.g. configure your announce address. This will be key in getting easy NAT traversal working for Logos Storage.
* We've dug into the Logos Blockchain Poseidon2 hash implementation, discovered a few issues, and [helped address them](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-blockchain/issues/2664).
* We have [implemented and documented](https://github.com/logos-storage/transport-over-mix/) a more general version of the Sphinx packet format as part of our effort in providing a reference implementation for our transport layer over Mix. We've also created [Haskell bindings](https://github.com/logos-storage/hs-leopard/) for the Leopard erasure coding library for that same reason -- transport layer will use erasure coding for space-efficient redundancy.

### [NAT Traversal](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/5)

    - Finalise the UDP experiment: most of the NAT seem to be `Address and Port-Dependent Filter` (APDF) as mentioned by [this paper](https://inria.hal.science/hal-01597969v1/document). The consequence is a client mode is needed for the DHT.
    - Update the [NAT Traversal strategy](https://hackmd.io/3DR2Of1qS9iwXl_qdGdkKg) to address the feedback and describe the strategy to retrieve the public IP.

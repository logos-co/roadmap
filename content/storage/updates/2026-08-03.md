## Week 31 2026 (Mon Jul 27 - Fri Jul 31, 2026)

### Highlights

* Progress on [hidden services over mix](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/20): studied and experimented with mix path selection approaches from recent research papers ([1](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3564625.3567996), [2](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-f2384-paper.pdf)) to address the malicious path problem in both anonymous download and hidden services.
* Published [work-in-progress notes](https://hackmd.io/@codex-storage/rJ4d1aaHfe) and [experiment code](https://github.com/logos-storage/hs-mix-sim) from the mix path selection experimentation, setting up the next step of writing specs for the transport layer.
* Evaluated libp2p's Kademlia DHT for the [anonymous DHT queries](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/3) effort, paving the way to replace the current discv5 DHT.

## Week 23 2026 (Mon June 01 - Fri June 05, 2026)

### Highlights

* After identifying a critical vulnerability with our initial hidden service specification, an second iteration is [now ready for review](https://github.com/logos-co/nim-libp2p-mix/pull/12).

* We gave a talk during this week's ZK research call about "dynamic" proofs; i.e., proofs in which a large part of the witness remains unchanged across proofs, and techniques for speeding those up. Writeup [available here](https://hackmd.io/@bkomuves/HyBL5V5xMe).

### [Hidden Services Over Mix](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/20)

- **achieved:**
    - Found (and hopefully fixed) an attack against hidden services with the current SURB-based design.
    - Worked on second iteration on the [hidden services specification](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/330):
        - Define hidden services epoch and update the descriptor and KDF used to match the [implementation](https://github.com/logos-storage/mix-hidden-services).
        - Changed the SURBs-based client-service communication and defined an approach that uses one-time rendezvous point since the previous approach turn out to be unsafe.
- **next:**
    - Studying the design choices and possible alternatives to (the various components of) the Tor design.

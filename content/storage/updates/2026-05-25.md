## Week 21 2026 (Mon May 18 - Fri May 22, 2026)

### Highlights

* Our cryptographers came back with new ideas from the conference week last week. Balazs is working on a lead for speeding up RLN, which could bring proof times down to 10ms, a 6x improvement over the current, already optimized implementation. Given the critical role of RLN on mix performance, this would be no small feat. It is based on [this paper](https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/1897).
* Our [LIONESS cipher spec](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/329) has finally been merged and closed, after a final round of reviews.
* Our first tests with DHT queries over mix have been successful. We are now working on integrating discovery to make it operational on the storage network at large.
* The reference implementation for the Ed25519 key blinding that we are planning to deploy in our future hidden-services [is now available](https://github.com/logos-storage/mix-hidden-services).
* We are making solid progress with NAT traversal, and should have a complete implementation covering the full range of options - from port mapped routers to UPnP/PCP/PMP to relay fallbacks - available for testing within a week or two.

## Week 30 2026 (Mon Jul 20 - Fri Jul 24, 2026)

### Highlights

* We made improvements to the Mix build system [making it more predictable](https://github.com/logos-co/nim-libp2p-mix/pull/33). This is part of the groundwork for the upcoming Mix transport layer.
* We have implemented three simulations from the ["When Mixnets Fail"](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-f2384-paper.pdf) paper in our [free-route mix simulator](https://github.com/logos-storage/hs-mix-sim) as we investigate how to work around the malicious path problem.
* Plans for the new storage docs [are complete](https://hackmd.io/@codex-storage/rJNzTdOQzl).

### [Hidden Services Over Mix](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/20)

- **achieved:**
    - studied and experimented with the different strategies for path selection from the paper: [When Mixnets Fail](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-f2384-paper.pdf). This provides more insight into the malicious path problem and possible solutions to limit it for both the anonnymous download and hidden services setting. 
    - worked on simulating the 3 path selection strategies from the paper in our [free-route mix simulator](https://github.com/logos-storage/hs-mix-sim).
- **next:**
    - study/experiment further with the path selection strategies, especially for our planned anonymous download and transport layer setting.
    - work on a proposal for a path selection strategy for anonymous download to limit the malicious path problem.
    - long term: investigate how these can possibly be extended and applied in the hidden services setting.

## Week 45 2026 (Mon Nov 2 - Sun Nov 8, 2026)

### Highlights

- We have our first official release of [MixTransport v0.1.0-beta.1](https://github.com/logos-storage/libp2p-mix-transport/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta.1), with performance improvements over initial releases that might make it up to 20% faster.
- The standalone implementation for our [dynamic Groth16 proofs](https://github.com/logos-storage/dynamic-groth16), which allows intermediate results to be reused across proofs greatly reducing amortized costs, is now available. It should be available to [ZeroKit](https://github.com/vacp2p/zerokit) users soon.

### [Large Data Transport Layer for Mix](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/2)

- **achieved:**
  - Merged [MixTransport](https://github.com/logos-storage/libp2p-mix-transport/pull/1) and released [v0.1.0-beta.1](https://github.com/logos-storage/libp2p-mix-transport/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta.1), providing multiplexed bidirectional byte streams over Mix, with ordered delivery, retransmission, backpressure and SURB supply management.
  - Made [concurrent redundant reverse Data copies](https://github.com/logos-storage/libp2p-mix-transport/pull/5) the default, removing the experimental compile flag. Control copies remain sequential.
  - Updated the [low-level Mix API](https://github.com/logos-co/nim-libp2p-mix/pull/47) for libp2p 2.4.1 and independent SURB reply consumption. [Pinned the prerelease](https://github.com/logos-storage/libp2p-mix-transport/pull/6) to that API revision and prepared the [Storage dependency update](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-nim/pull/1578).
  - [PR47](https://github.com/logos-co/nim-libp2p-mix/pull/47) is open for review (Prem is working on it)
- **next:**
  - Complete upstream Mix API integration and land the Storage dependency update after the [libp2p 2.4.1 prerequisite](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-nim/pull/1546).

### [Block Protocol and Mix Transport Optimizations](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/33)

- **achieved:**

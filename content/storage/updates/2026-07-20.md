## Week 29 2026 (Mon Jul 13 - Fri Jul 17, 2026)

### Highlights

* The [Hidden Services over Mix](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/20) work landed a free-route mix [(`freeroutesim`)](https://github.com/logos-storage/hs-mix-sim/tree/main/crates/freeroutesim) simulator for studying the path-selection and malicious-path problem in our mix protocol, along with improved documentation of the simulator and its results.
* Progress continues on [Anonymous DHT Queries](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/3), with all wire protocols migrated from `minprotobuf` to `nim-protobuf-serialization`.
* The [Integration with Logos Core](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-nim/milestone/10) effort extended several Logos Design System components - [buttons](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-design-system/pull/30), [textfields](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-design-system/pull/31), [frames](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-design-system/pull/32), and [button icons](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-design-system/pull/35) - and aligned the Logos Storage UI with the design system.

### [Hidden Services Over Mix](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/20)

- **achieved:**
    - completed the implementation of the free-route mix [(`freeroutesim`)](https://github.com/logos-storage/hs-mix-sim/tree/main/crates/freeroutesim) simulator for simulating the path selection and malicious-path problem. This helps us study the problem better in the mix protocol that we are using.
    - improved documentation of the simulator and results.
- **next:**
    - Continue research and study the problem in the context of anonymous download using the transport layer since the problem still exists there.

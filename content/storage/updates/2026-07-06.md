## Week 27 2026 (Mon Jun 29 - Fri Jul 3, 2026)

### Highlights

* Maintenance releases for the [Logos Storage Module](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-storage-module/releases/tag/v2.0.1) and the [Logos Storage UI](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-storage-ui/releases/tag/v2.0.1), which ships support for DHT queries over mix.

This, together with what we have released [last week](./2026-06-29.md), completes the round of deliverables for the Logos v0.2 testnet.


### [Hidden Services Over Mix](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/20)

- **achieved:**
    - Studied the [guard](https://spec.torproject.org/guard-spec/index.html) and [vanguard](https://spec.torproject.org/vanguards-spec/) specs that Tor uses to get a better understanding of the malicious path problem.
    - Experimented with the guard approach over mix as described in the [paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3564625.3567996).

### [Integration with Logos Core](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-nim/milestone/10)

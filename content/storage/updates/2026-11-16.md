## Week 46 2026 (Mon Nov 9 - Sun Nov 15, 2026)

> Evidence covers the week through Sunday, November 15, in America/Sao_Paulo. Meeting-based progress and next steps are tentative. Sources include the November 10 research notes and November 11 Gemini engineering notes; full meeting transcripts were unavailable.

### Highlights

- The first [MixTransport DHT proxy implementation](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-nim/pull/1581) lets a proxy return provider lists larger than a single Mix packet. Storage also gained [service discovery](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-nim/pull/1582) for DHT Mix proxies, Mix relays, circuit relays and AutoNAT servers.
- The Storage Module merged [network presets](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-storage-module/pull/105) that load bootstrap and Mix settings generated from the fleet configuration.
- Dynamic Groth16 v2 gained [libsnark support and tests](https://github.com/logos-storage/dynamic-groth16/commit/f33f9a784a7b3f8cbe393c2c50e5cf6e83307fc2). The [ZeroKit integration branch](https://github.com/mghazwi/zerokit/tree/dynamic-groth16) now supports both Circom and libsnark reductions.

### [Large Data Transport Layer for Mix](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/2)

- **achieved:**
  - logos-storage: keeping [#1578](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-nim/pull/1578) and [#1546](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-nim/pull/1546) in ready to merge stage by aligning with upstream changes
  - logos-co: keeping [#47](https://github.com/logos-co/nim-libp2p-mix/pull/47) ready to merge
- **next:**
  - Finalize the MixTransport specification, reconcile it with the original transport design, and submit it to the LIPs repository.

### [Use Mix Transport for DHT Proxies](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/34)

- **achieved:**
  - Prepared the [first MixTransport-based DHT proxy implementation](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-nim/pull/1581), replacing the single-packet exchange with reliable streams terminating at the proxy. Added bounded requests and responses, timeout/cancellation handling, and tests covering responses larger than the old packet budget while preserving no direct client-side DHT fallback.
- **next:**
  - Assess and validate [integration with service discovery](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/45), including proxy identity, Mix addressing and failure handling.

### [Use Service Discovery](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/32)

- **achieved:**
  - Implemented [discovery of AutoNAT servers, circuit relays, Mix relays and DHT Mix proxies](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-nim/pull/1582), retaining static configuration and adding a DHT-proxy server flag.
  - Investigated long registration delays when several services share an IP address. Friday's implementation [advertises all services under one discovery ID](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-nim/commit/57cefa0c1e437640ab8d72c2287bdfc48e0208cf) and disables the IP-similarity coefficient.
- **next:**
  - Clarify the registration-delay and anti-Sybil tradeoffs with the protocol maintainers, and validate discovery together with MixTransport proxy lookups.

### [Block Protocol and Mix Transport Optimizations](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/33)

- **achieved:**
  - Merged the [swarm availability and peer-replacement fix](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-nim/pull/1577), retaining availability needed by a download and triggering discovery when no peer covers its current batch.
  - Prepared [background-download outcome reporting](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-nim/pull/1580): progress and cancellation find downloads by manifest or tree CID, and progress retains completed, failed and cancelled outcomes instead of treating every inactive download alike.

### [Logos Core Integration](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/40)

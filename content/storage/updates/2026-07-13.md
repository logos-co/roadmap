## Week 28 2026 (Mon Jul 06 - Fri Jul 10, 2026)

### Highlights

* The Logos storage UI landed [configuration versioning](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-storage-ui/pull/68) support, alongside [documentation improvements](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-docs/pull/392) following a UI/UX sync with Aqeel.
* nim-libplum now [exposes an API to filter the protocol](https://github.com/logos-storage/nim-libplum/pull/15), with a move back to upstream paullouisageneau/libplum planned once its [PR](https://github.com/paullouisageneau/libplum/pull/56) lands.
* General hardening continued across the stack: [static libstorage artefacts](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-nim/pull/1488) now ship with releases, a [UPnP crash](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-nim/pull/1486) was fixed, [shutdown handling](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-nim/pull/1487) was improved, and the [Go bindings](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-go-bindings/pull/27) were updated.

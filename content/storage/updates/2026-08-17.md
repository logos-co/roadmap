## Week 33 2026 (Mon Aug 10 - Fri Aug 14, 2026)

### Highlights

* We [published a research post](https://forum.research.logos.co/t/mix-path-selection/721) on mix path selection for anonymized traffic, part of our ongoing work on [hidden services over mix](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/20).
* Completed an [evaluation of anonymous downloads over the transport layer](https://github.com/logos-storage/hs-mix-sim/tree/main/crates/freeroutesim), simulating the proposed path-selection approach.
* Advanced the [draft spec for mix path selection](https://hackmd.io/@codex-storage/HyU6451wGl), which will be shared by both anonymous downloads and hidden services.

### [Hidden Services Over Mix](https://github.com/logos-storage/logos-storage-pm/issues/20)

- **achieved:**
    - completed the [research post on mix path selection](https://forum.research.logos.co/t/mix-path-selection/721) and their [evaluation for the anonymous download](https://github.com/logos-storage/hs-mix-sim/tree/main/crates/freeroutesim) over transport layer. 
    - worked on the [draft spec](https://hackmd.io/@codex-storage/HyU6451wGl) for mix path selection to be used for both anonymous download and hidden services.

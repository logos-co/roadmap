## `blockchain:bedrock:research`

- `blockchain:bedrock:research:cryptarchia`
  - SDP RFCs merged: declarations identified by service and `zk_id` [logos-lips#407](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/407), minimum stake held as one MinStake [logos-lips#498](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/498)
  - Eras RFC (in-review) [logos-lips#439](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/439): per-era rules for the Proof of Work window, fork-choice k, uncle window, and minimum stake; mempool follows the eras
  - Mempool retention reworked (in-review) [logos-lips#448](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/448): retention constant replaced by the retirement grace counted from the one-hour TTL, recovery rebuilds included transactions from stored blocks; mempool spec covers the ten-minute retirement grace and node-set TTL (in-review) [logos-lips#413](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/413)
  - New RFCs: uncle parents kept below the latest immutable block (draft) [logos-lips#509](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/509); responsive peers preferred for block downloads (draft) [logos-lips#504](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/504); unreachable peers removed from the routing table (draft) [logos-lips#505](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/505); orphan LIB check of chain sync compares slots (in-review) [logos-lips#507](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/507)
  - Execution Gas measured component by component (in-review) [logos-lips#506](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/506), implemented with gas cost benchmarks in core cycles (in-review) [#3922](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-blockchain/pull/3922); block nullifiers hashed once and SDP uniqueness looked up by key (in-review) [#3921](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-blockchain/pull/3921)

- `blockchain:bedrock:research:blend`
  - Blend liveness RFCs merged: observation window sized by cover traffic, 30 rounds at reference parameters and 90 on testnet [logos-lips#499](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/499); a not-live neighbor refused for W rounds, then its count restarts [logos-lips#500](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/500)
  - M_W derived from expected deliveries (in-review) [logos-lips#510](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/510), implemented (in-review) [#3965](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-blockchain/pull/3965)

- `blockchain:bedrock:research:cryptoeconomics`
  - LEPTON defined as the indivisible token unit (in-review) [logos-lips#503](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/503)

- `blockchain:bedrock:research:empowering`
  - Mining-funded onboarding model extended past one year with delayed unlocking of mined tokens; tighter qualification bounds with leadership rewards included
  - Numerical experiments on arrival schedules, mining rates, leadership income, and the trade-off between onboarding speed and reward-pool depletion; analytical solutions checked against ODE simulations
  - Expected mining income related to hash rate and difficulty, including the limits of an average hash rate

- `blockchain:bedrock:research:post-quantum`
  - Post-quantum note encryption RFC through its first review round; open points: ciphertext on-chain or on Data Availability or storage, KEM size, hash choice (in-review) [logos-lips#486](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/486); test vectors for note encryption and the new transaction encoding started, cross-checked against two independent implementations
  - Post-quantum transport RFC rebased with the Blend spec aligned to hybrid-only key exchange (in-review) [logos-lips#429](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/429); next testnet to ship hybrid post-quantum TLS

## `blockchain:bedrock:research`

- `blockchain:bedrock:research:cryptarchia`
  - Strict EdDSA verification spec merged: weak keys rejected in inscription operations [logos-lips#461](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/461); gas figures follow-up (in-review) [logos-lips#464](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/464)
  - Hierarchical deterministic wallet: HD path [#3617](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-blockchain/pull/3617) and mnemonic [#3618](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-blockchain/pull/3618) primitives merged

- `blockchain:bedrock:research:empowering`
  - Mining-funded onboarding model extended from a fixed genesis population to newcomer cohorts arriving every epoch, with token unlocking after one year: analytical stake and unlocked-balance trajectories, qualification conditions, and minimum mining incomes derived; the delay incumbent mining imposes on newcomer qualification quantified [doc](https://www.overleaf.com/read/xqzvgkxbcvhn#75248b)
  - Interactive onboarding calculator published: mining income needed for a target qualification deadline, with stake, unlocked-balance, and reward-pool plots and scenario comparisons [calculator](https://amozeika.github.io/onboarding-calculator/)

- `blockchain:bedrock:research:post-quantum`
  - Post-quantum, statistically secure Data Availability study part 1 opened: options compared, one-dimensional ZODA check row recommended (draft) [research#19](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/research/pull/19)

- `blockchain:bedrock:research:permissionless-channel-sequencing`
  - Holder-authorized channel notes RFC opened: users keep ownership of channel funds, sequencer power over funds removed (in-review) [logos-lips#457](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/457)

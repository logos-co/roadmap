- `blockchain:bedrock:research:cryptarchia`
  - Mantle operation data payloads bounded RFC merged [logos-lips#467](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/467)
  - Private UTXO: programs governing channel notes RFC (in-review) [logos-lips#481](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/481), stacked on the private UTXO ledger RFC [logos-lips#480](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/480); private ledger circuits for transfer, program spend, Proof of Leadership and Proof of Quota (in-review) [logos-blockchain-circuits#59](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/logos-blockchain-circuits/pull/59); Mantle and token economics formalised in Lean (in-review) [research#25](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/research/pull/25)
  - Off-chain inscription bodies on Logos Storage: first measurements on the live storage network show the protocol is fast enough for Logos Execution Zone sized payloads, three storage-side issues found (draft) [logos-lips#454](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/454)

- `blockchain:bedrock:research:empowering`
  - Mining-funded onboarding model consolidated into a standalone document [doc](https://www.overleaf.com/read/qmfpfvgjxgmp#1baf68); fixed total mining payout per epoch analysed for onboarding times, pool dynamics, and newcomer arrivals [slides](https://www.overleaf.com/read/hgrsgbfrpvqm#e322fd)
  - Constant per-miner rewards with mining expenses: qualification-time bound and pool expenditure bounds derived, one- and two-week qualification targets evaluated for 3,500 newcomers and 100 incumbent miners
  - Block-rewards baseline rebuilt: its 1.33% holds only without recycled fees (in-review) [research#24](https://github.com/logos-blockchain/research/pull/24)

- `blockchain:bedrock:research:post-quantum`

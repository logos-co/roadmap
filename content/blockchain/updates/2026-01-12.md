# Logos Blockchain Weekly Update - 2026-01-12

## `blockchain:highlights`
- Work has begun on turning Logos Execution Environment into a proper Zone running on the Logos Blockchain
- Unification of Public and Private execution inside of Logos Execution Environment will provide a more consistent experience for developers in building applications using private or public state.
- Published our 2025 Year in Review [blog](https://blog.nomos.tech/2025-year-in-review/).

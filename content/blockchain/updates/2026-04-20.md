## `blockchain:bedrock:research`

- `blockchain:bedrock:research:total-stake-inference`
  - Derived variance approximation for total stake estimator: proportional to network delay variance, inversely proportional to mean delay; variance no longer maximized by ideal scenario [draft](https://www.notion.so/nomos-tech/Total-Stake-Inference-Analysis-counting-factor-and-blockchain-evidence-f82261aa09df8253b32d01fe395f6ec7)
  - Convergence analysis: identified three regimes based on E[Delta] vs 1/f; derived optimal learning rate h=1/f balancing stability, robustness, and simplicity
  - TSI Analysis draft: Sections 1-5 rewritten with unified notation and improved readability; appendix reorganized
  - Improved "Total Active Stake Inference under Stake Privacy" paper [paper](https://www.notion.so/nomos-tech/Total-Active-Stake-Inference-under-Stake-Privacy-310261aa09df8069afe2de5c0a97231d)

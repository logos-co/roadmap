# Blockchain PoS Participation for Bridged Funds

Users who bridge tokens into a Sovereign Zone can still keep a native token representation of those funds on the blockchain, and use that representation to participate in Proof of Stake. Bridged funds keep contributing to chain security instead of lying dormant.

## Resources Required

- 1 Researcher
- 2 Engineers

## Risks

- Staking with the blockchain representation must stay consistent with bridge withdrawals, so the same funds cannot be staked and withdrawn at the same time

## Highlights

- The [mix key rotation specification](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/blob/master/docs/anoncomms/raw/mix-key-rotation.md) has been merged along with its library implementation, and rotation is being [integrated into Logos Delivery](https://github.com/logos-messaging/logos-delivery/pull/4812).
- The RLN Membership Zone design, which aims to make RLN memberships cheap enough for routine use, has been submitted as a [raw specification](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/548).
- A [sponsor relay](https://github.com/logos-messaging/account-registry-lez/pull/14) for the λAccount registry now lets users publish account logs without paying fees themselves.

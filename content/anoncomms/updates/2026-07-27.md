## Highlights

- The [oracle mechanism specification](https://lip.logos.co/anoncomms/raw/logos-oracle-zone.html) is now published as a LIP, laying the groundwork for the Decentralised Oracle Network.
- Mix-based chat with DoS protection was presented and demoed at the [IFT Town Hall](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6TRcUIYt_wE&list=PLZe53tXAogqNiGROz9NvxTJ251sK1TiLH&index=1).

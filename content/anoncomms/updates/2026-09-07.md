## Highlights

- Published the [Logos Mix Network LIP](https://lip.logos.co/anoncomms/raw/logos-mixnet.html), specifying the Logos-specific mix protocol stack and its opinionated configuration.
- Completed the [Poseidon2 circuit implementation](https://github.com/vacp2p/circom-rln/pull/1) in Circom with a benchmark report, and added [Poseidon2 examples and FFI/WASM support](https://github.com/vacp2p/zerokit/pull/437) to Zerokit.
- The standalone service discovery module now plugs into Logos Delivery, with the [discovery plugin header PR](https://github.com/logos-messaging/logos-delivery/pull/4178) opened and verified locally against Delivery nodes.

## Highlights

- Mix key rotation for forward secrecy is taking shape, with a [spec PR](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/502) and a first [implementation PR](https://github.com/logos-co/nim-libp2p-mix/pull/78) covering the key ring and candidate-key processing.
- The Logos Oracle Network design now includes a dispute mechanism with an end-to-end flow, published for discussion in a [forum post](https://forum.research.logos.co/t/logos-oracle-network-lon-request-for-a-call/738) and the updated [specification PR](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/390).
- A combined Mix and Delivery module [architecture](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-delivery-module/issues/183) has been proposed, enabling the Logos Mix module to act as entry and exit node for Logos Delivery.

## Highlights

- [Zerokit v3.0.0](https://github.com/vacp2p/zerokit/releases/tag/v3.0.0) has been released — the Rust zero-knowledge library powering RLN across Logos and Status now ships with enum-based runtime configuration.
- Published [RLN-based DoS protection for libp2p Mix: four constructions](https://forum.research.logos.co/t/rln-based-dos-protection-for-libp2p-mix-four-constructions/720), comparing four approaches to rate-limiting mixnet traffic without compromising anonymity.
- The [Payment Streams specification](https://lip.logos.co/anoncomms/raw/payment-streams.html) now covers non-native token support in provider payment policies.

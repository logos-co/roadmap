## Highlights

- Zerokit [v3.1.0](https://github.com/vacp2p/zerokit/releases/tag/v3.1.0) has been released, adding support for the Poseidon2 hash function.
- The dispute mechanism for the Logos Oracle Network has been merged into the [LON specification](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/blob/master/docs/anoncomms/raw/logos-oracle-zone.md).
- Mix key rotation on a local timer is [implemented](https://github.com/logos-co/nim-libp2p-mix/pull/91) following the updated [spec](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-lips/pull/527), bringing forward secrecy to the libp2p mixnet.

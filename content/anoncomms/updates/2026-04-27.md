## Highlights

- Cover traffic generation for the libp2p mix protocol merged into nim-libp2p ([PR #2243](https://github.com/vacp2p/nim-libp2p/pull/2243)), with spec comments addressed and a configurable cover-ratio-fraction option added ([PR #2322](https://github.com/vacp2p/nim-libp2p/pull/2322)).
- [Payment streams](https://github.com/logos-co/lez-payment-streams) for an MVP payment protocol over LEZ are mostly implemented for public execution mode, with private execution adaptation next.
- A new [protocol](https://lip.logos.co/ift-ts/raw/rln-membership-service.html) allowing RLN membership distribution based on authentication has been merged as a new LIP.

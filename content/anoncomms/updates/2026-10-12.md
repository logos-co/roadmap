## Highlights

- Service discovery [benchmark results](https://zealous-polka-dc7.notion.site/Service-discovery-analysis-39d8f96fb65c8098864bdfe91b012abf) confirm that a rare service offered by only 1 in 200 nodes is consistently discovered in under 1.5 seconds.
- The service discovery module was validated end to end as a Logos Delivery plugin, running on the test fleet with deployed module artifacts.
- A first draft of the RLN Membership Zone RFC, with an example implementation, is available in the new [logos-rln-membership-zone](https://github.com/logos-co/logos-rln-membership-zone) repository.

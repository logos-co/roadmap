## Highlights

- A finality extension for SDS to support de-MLS commit ordering has been proposed, with a [draft spec](https://github.com/seugu/majority-observed-total-ordering/blob/main/sds-finality-spec.md) and [reference repo](https://github.com/seugu/majority-observed-total-ordering) open for discussion.
- The RLN program now supports different rate limits per instance, and the RLN module and membership allocation service can manage memberships across multiple registries, paving the way for a second registry dedicated to mix memberships.

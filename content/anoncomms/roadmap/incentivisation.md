# Service Incentivisation Track

For many core Logos applications, incentivising the provision of services is necessary to establish a sustainable infrastructure.
In Logos Messaging protocols, for example, services like Store and Lightpush depends on our own infrastructure nodes or a few altruistic providers.
This is especially true for mobile devices that rely on services for resource-restricted access to various Logos functions.
This results in single points of failure and a vulnerability to censorship attacks.
The first step to incentivisation is to define a privacy-preserving payment protocol by which clients can pay specific service providers in a decentralised setting.

In this track, we specify and implement a privacy-preserving payment protocol for service incentivisation.

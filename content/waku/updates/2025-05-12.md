## Highlights

- nwaku: Deprecation of sync/local Merkle tree completed.

The Waku team met up in Split, Croatia for a team off-site, where they explored the development of the protocol, discussed ongoing research, and connected with peers.

This event also offered the opportunity for Waku to align with other IFT projects and work closely with the [Codex](https://codex.storage) and [Nomos](https://nomoos.tech) teams on building out and refining the [Logos](https://logos.co) tech stack.

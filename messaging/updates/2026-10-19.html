Delivery — Technical Debt Halved

  • [Deliverable] Close baseline technical debt issues
    • achieved:
      • move the codecs to proto3 and retire minprotobuf (#4488)
      • bound the size of store, lightpush and sync reads (#4489)
      • deprecate the archive capacity retention policy (#4593)
      • relay, sharding and CLI conformance:
        • don’t hand a duplicate publish to own handlers again (#4519)
        • content-topic unsubscribe keeps other topics on the shard (#4518)
        • subscribe to configured shards on start under static sharding (#4516)
        • --shard limits the relay shards under autosharding (#4513)
        • refuse shards of another cluster (#4514)
        • stop mapping --cluster-id=2 to the logos.dev preset (#4515)
        • let --preset set max-msg-size (#4567)
        • keep topic health in sync across stop/start (#4568)
        • relay a message encoded at the max message size (#4578)
        • keep connected peers out of CannotConnect (#4512)
      • non-My getNodeInfo ids with the old ones as deprecated aliases (#4577, module#168)

Store Sync — DST Signed Off

  • [Deliverable] Review implementation of Store Sync protocol
    • achieved:
      • follow the WAKU-SYNC wire formats (#4571)
      • clamp the ranges of sessions started by a peer (#4535) and of our own to the time retention (#4478)
      • transfer reads up to --max-msg-size and reset oversized sessions (#4547)
      • skip messages a peer refused as too large (#4562)
      • let a stop end the transfer loop (#4551)
      • create the partitions for the hours before the node start (#4524)

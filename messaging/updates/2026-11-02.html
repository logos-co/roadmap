Store Sync — DST Signed Off

  • [Deliverable] Review implementation of Store Sync protocol

    • achieved:
      • fix(store-sync): encode and decode same-timestamp bounds against the previous bound, as WAKU-SYNC defines (#4621, closes #4574).

      • Fix #4657: split store sync’s processRequest into smaller helpers (#4659).

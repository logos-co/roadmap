Nomos Weekly Update - 2025-04-07 §

Nomos Bedrock §

Research §

  • Finality Gadget research:
    • Progress on the design of two-level finality proposal
    • Further study of “safe” Ghost variant and Peras for finality gadget design
    • Committee sampling algorithm investigation continues
  • Network protocol research:
    • Preliminary investigation for p2p core network peer discovery and membership
    • NAT traversal considerations draft in progress
  • Mantle Zone development:
    • Complete refactor of the Mantle Zone Specification
    • Introduction of unshielded note set to support staking/slashing
    • Transaction model and gas model for Mantle transaction fees established
    • Method for pricing blobs by usage developed
  • Nomos Economic Model development:

    • Blockspace Market for Nomos ready for team review
